HANGAR HOVER / NIGHT WATCH

Extract this folder, then open index.html in a browser.
Tap Touch to start. Art and audio preload in the background while you
use the menu. Choose Continue to load a campaign checkpoint or New game
to start in one of three save slots. Older saves appear in slot 1.
The Menu button pauses an active flight. Back to game returns to it.
Progress is saved on this device in the current browser.

OPTIONS
Normal is the default. Easy adds 30% battery time, softens wind and
signal drain, and makes scanning and landing more forgiving in Story
and Practice. A difficulty change takes effect at the next launch.
Touch sensitivity adjusts joystick response. Options also controls audio.

FLIGHT
WASD or arrows   Steer
Space           Brake and steady the drone
C               Jam red cameras for 2.5 seconds. Three uses per flight.
P               Pause or resume
R               Restart the current mission

Stay inside each numbered reticle until its scan finishes. Green camera
coverage speeds up scans. Red coverage drains battery faster. Collect
purple battery cells for up to four seconds of extra flight time, then
hold a steady hover over the extraction pad after recording every target.
In the final Gale Warehouse mission, fly east through the open loading door.

MISSIONS, HANGAR, AND UPGRADES
Use the top buttons to switch pages. Use Previous and Next to page through practice missions.
Mission clears earn credits for upgrades to the selected drone.

PROJECT FILES
index.html    Page structure
styles.css    Layout and embedded display font
game.js       Flight physics, course layouts, artwork rendering, and progression
assets/       ImageGen sprite atlas, synthesized soundtrack, and seven effects

All gameplay and assets run locally. Browser storage saves upgrades and times
when available. The atlas is a transparent 3 × 3 sheet. Sprite rectangles
are listed in game.js. The display font license is included in index.html.

STORY AND PRACTICE
Story is the main campaign. Fly all 36 missions in order across three 12-mission cases. Every three
missions unlock a chapter reveal. The final epilogue includes the case
outcome, thank-you slides, and production credits. Practice offers cleared maps and your current Story mission
without advancing the story. Campaign progress is saved separately from
practice clears and credits. The Gold Aurel G7 and Blue Vector B9 are
available immediately in the Hangar. Dialogue lives in story.js. North Quay maps live in north-quay.js.

RACE
Your rival and course stay assigned until you win, including after a reload.
After a win, the game draws a different pilot and a fresh course inspired by
the story maps. Gale Warehouse occasionally brings crosswinds.
Clear four checkpoints and cross the east shutter. A win pays 110 CR, and a
completed loss pays 35 CR. The rival drone can overlap yours safely.
Each upgrade now has five tiers.

Sound adds the original spy-jazz score, reactive rotor hum, and distinct
throttle, brake, landing, and race-win effects.

PHONE CONTROLS
Drag the left joystick in any direction. A short pull applies gentle
thrust and a full pull applies maximum thrust. Hold Brake with your
right thumb while steering, or tap Jam to disable cameras briefly.
In landscape, the controls move beside the flight deck.

NORTH QUAY
After Sable Freight, continue to Case 02 at a shuttered ferry warehouse.
Cyan fenders and foam bumpers rebound on gentle contact. Hard impacts
still end the flight. The Kestrel N12 costs 450 credits and the Breaker
X14 costs 700 credits. Once purchased, a frame stays yours.
